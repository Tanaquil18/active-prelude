Preview Activity 2.4.1.

Let \(f(t) = \cos(t)\text{.}\) First, answer all of the questions below without using Desmos; then use Desmos to confirm your conjectures. For each prompt, describe the graphs of \(g\) and \(h\) as transformations of \(f\) and, in addition, state the amplitude, midline, and period of both \(g\) and \(h\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

(a)

\(g(t) = 3\cos(t)\) and \(h(t) = -\frac{1}{4}\cos(t)\)
🔗
Solution.
\(g(t)\) is a vertical stretch of \(f\) by a factor of 3, with amplitude 3, midline \(y=0\text{,}\) and period \(2\pi\text{.}\) \(h(t)\) is a vertical shrink by a factor of \(\frac{1}{4}\) combined with a reflection over the \(x\)-axis, with amplitude \(\frac{1}{4}\text{,}\) midline \(y=0\text{,}\) and period \(2\pi\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
🔗

(b)

\(g(t) = \cos(t-\pi)\) and \(h(t) = \cos\left(t+ \frac{\pi}{2}\right)\)
🔗
Solution.
\(g(t)\) is a horizontal translation of \(f\) by \(\pi\) units to the right (equivalently, a reflection over the \(x\)-axis due to periodicity), with amplitude 1, midline \(y=0\text{,}\) and period \(2\pi\text{.}\) \(h(t)\) is a horizontal translation \(\frac{\pi}{2}\) units to the left, with amplitude 1, midline \(y=0\text{,}\) and period \(2\pi\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
🔗

(c)

\(g(t) = \cos(t)+4\) and \(h(t) = \cos\left(t\right)-2\)
🔗
Solution.
\(g(t)\) is a vertical translation of \(f\) by 4 units up, with amplitude 1, midline \(y=4\text{,}\) and period \(2\pi\text{.}\) \(h(t)\) is a vertical translation 2 units down, with amplitude 1, midline \(y=-2\text{,}\) and period \(2\pi\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
🔗

(d)

\(g(t) = 3\cos(t-\pi)+4\) and \(h(t) = -\frac{1}{4}\cos\left(t+ \frac{\pi}{2}\right)-2\)
🔗
Solution.
\(g(t)\) is a vertical stretch by a factor of 3, followed by a horizontal shift \(\pi\) units to the right, and a vertical shift 4 units up. Due to the periodicity, it is also accurate to say that \(g(t)\) is reflected over the \(x\)-axis, stretched by a factor of 3, and shifted vertically up 4 units. \(g(t)\) has amplitude 3, midline \(y=4\text{,}\) and period \(2\pi\text{.}\)
🔗
\(h(t)\) is a reflection over the \(x\)-axis, followed by a vertical shrink by a factor of \(\frac{1}{4}\text{,}\) then a horizontal shift \(\frac{\pi}{2}\) units to the left, and a vertical shift 2 units down. Due to the periodicity, it is also accurate to say that \(h(t)\) is shifted \(\frac{\pi}{2}\) units to the right, shrunk by a factor of \(\frac{1}{4}\text{,}\) and shifted 2 units down. \(h(t)\) has amplitude \(\frac{1}{4}\text{,}\) midline \(y=-2\text{,}\) and period \(2\pi\text{.}\)
